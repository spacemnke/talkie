Do not disturb

Talkie waits in the menu bar. When a friend patches in, the icon lights, the mic drops onto the screen, and their voice comes straight through. Muting someone is private: they never see it.

1How a call works

From plug to hang-up, both ends.

1

Plug in

Drop a cord into a friend's jack. Talkie sends one message to the operator: Sam patched Maya.

2

Line opens

The line opens at once and a mic drops onto both screens, floating above every app. No ringing, no answer button. If Maya is on do not disturb, the patch waits.

3

Talk

Hold the mic or a hotkey, from any app. You can both talk at the same time, like a phone call. Maya can mute you without you knowing.

4

Pull the plug

Unplugging hangs up on both ends. The mic winds back up into the menu bar.

2Voice FX

Pick one here or press the orange key on the mic, then squeeze to hear it.

Effects run on your Mac before your voice is sent, so your friend hears them too. In the app: AVAudioEngine's delay, EQ, distortion and pitch units, plus a ring modulator for Machine.

3What's in the box

Four parts, all built from tools that already exist.

4Decisions so far

Three settled, two still open.

Instant line

A patch opens the line straight away. That's what sets Talkie apart. Do not disturb holds patches until you're back.

Decided

Both talk at once

Full duplex, like a phone call. Nobody waits for "over".

Decided

Mute is private

When Maya mutes you, your board doesn't show it. Her lamp looks the same either way.

Decided

Hear yourself?

Hearing your own Machine voice is half the fun of effects, but through speakers it can feed back into the mic. Maybe headphones only.

Open

iPhone too?

iOS has Apple's Push to Talk framework, which can deliver a friend's voice while the phone is locked. The Mac app comes first either way.
