How a call works

Knock, talk, unplug

  1. 1Knocking

    Knock

    Drag a cord into a friend's jack. They hear a knock and their mic drops with your name on it. Your lamp blinks until they let you in, then stays orange. If they'd rather not, you just get no answer.

  2. 2On the line

    Talk

    Once you're in, the mic drops out of the menu bar and floats above your apps. Hold it, or hold ⌥ Space from any app, and talk. You can both talk at the same time.

  3. 3Idle

    Pull the plug

    Unplug the cord and the line closes. The mic winds back up into the menu bar until the next call.

The mic

Out on a coiled cord while the line is open

When a friend knocks or a line opens, the mic drops from the menu bar and hangs over every app, full-screen ones included. Drag it or fling it anywhere and it stays where you leave it.

  • When a friend knocks, press Let in, or hold it to let them in and talk at once. The × turns them away, and they're never told.
  • Hold it, or hold ⌥ Space, to talk.
  • The orange side key changes your voice: Canyon, Tin can, Helium or Machine.
  • The grey side key buzzes everyone on the line.
  • The key on its other side has mute, do not disturb, effects and hang up.
  • It never shows up in screen shares or recordings.
  • Click the jack in the menu bar to put it away.

Adding friends

Eight jacks, one code each

Everyone on Talkie has a code, like TLK-7Q4F. When a friend sends you theirs, press ADD on your switchboard (or click a free jack) and type it in. They land on the jack, lamp and all.

Your board has eight jacks: three on Free, all eight on Pro. Right-click a jack to eject someone and make room; their code brings them back any time.

Send yours as a line card: a punched card with your name, your code and a hole pattern of its own.

Download Talkie.dmg Universal app · macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  1. Open Talkie.dmg and drag Talkie into Applications.
  2. Open Talkie. The first time, macOS stops it because it isn't notarized yet.
