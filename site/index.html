How a call works

Three moves, no dialing

  1. 1Connecting

    Patch in

    Drag a cord into a friend's jack. The lamp blinks while the line connects, then stays orange. It takes less than a second.

  2. 2On the line

    Talk

    The mic drops out of the menu bar and floats above your apps. Hold it, or hold ⌥ Space from any app, and talk. You can both talk at the same time.

  3. 3Idle

    Pull the plug

    Unplug the cord and the line closes. The mic winds back up into the menu bar until the next call.

The mic

Out on a coiled cord while the line is open

When a line opens, the mic drops from the menu bar and hangs over every app, full-screen ones included. Drag it or fling it anywhere and it stays where you leave it.

  • Hold it, or hold ⌥ Space, to talk.
  • The orange side key changes your voice: Canyon, Tin can, Helium or Machine.
  • The grey side key buzzes everyone on the line.
  • It never shows up in screen shares or recordings.
  • Click the jack in the menu bar to put it away.

Adding friends

Eight jacks and a Rolodex

Everyone on Talkie has a code, like TLK-7Q4F. When a friend sends you theirs, check it in the card file and the punch issues their line card. Drag the card into the punch and they land on a free jack.

Your board holds eight friends. Everyone you add keeps a card in the Rolodex, so you can eject someone to make room and punch their card back in later.

Each code works once. Every card has its own hole pattern.

Download Talkie.dmg Universal app · macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  1. Open Talkie.dmg and drag Talkie into Applications.
  2. Open Talkie. The first time, macOS stops it because it isn't notarized yet.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. Look for the jack in your menu bar. Click it to open the board.
